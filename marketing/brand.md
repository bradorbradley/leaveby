# Leave By brand voice (rules for every post, page and reply)

**Who we talk to:** people about to fly, skewing women 25–44, often the planner of the trip. They're anxious about one thing: when to leave.

**Voice:** calm, competent, a little funny. Speak like the friend who already checked. Short sentences. Name the real thing (the Van Wyck, Terminal 5, the bag-drop cutoff).

**The mascot** (the plush in the Hawaiian shirt) is our narrator and the face of the brand. It goes through the stress so the viewer doesn't have to. It never talks down.

## Hard rules
1. **Never encourage cutting it close.** Never suggest arriving after an airline's check-in, bag-drop or boarding cutoff. When we mention cutting it close, the point is that it fails.
2. **Real numbers only.** Every leave time shown comes from a real Leave By plan, labeled with the date and "yours will differ." Every statistic has a named source on the slide or in the caption.
3. **No fake testimonials.** Skits are labeled POV or use the mascot. We never claim a real person said or did something they didn't.
4. **No fear-mongering.** Stress is the setup; calm is the payoff.
5. **Label AI-generated video and images** with the platform's AI-content label where one exists.
6. **The call to action is always the same:** leaveby.xyz, free, no account, link in bio.

## Formats that we test
- **Airport theory, tested:** the TikTok trend versus a real plan.
- **POV skits:** "He says we can leave at 6:30…" then the real plan.
- **Data drops:** TSA records, EU passport waits, holiday peaks, always with a source.
- **Mascot story:** chaos versus calm, using stills from the ad.
- **Lists:** "4 things that make you miss your flight."

Each concept ships with two opening hooks on otherwise identical slides, so we learn which hook works.
